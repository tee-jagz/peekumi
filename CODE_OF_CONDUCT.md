# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We, the members, contributors and maintainers, make the participation in our community free of harassment for all persons. This applies to all persons. Their age, body size, disability (visible or invisible), ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, and level of experience do not matter. Their education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, and sexual identity and orientation also do not matter.

We act and speak in ways that help to make an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps the community:

- Show empathy and kindness to other people.
- Respect different opinions, points of view and experiences.
- Give constructive feedback, and accept it with grace.
- Accept responsibility for your mistakes, apologise to the persons that they affect, and learn from them.
- Think about what is best for the whole community, not only for you.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexual language or images, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publication of the private information of other persons, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that people can reasonably think is not appropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainers make these standards clear, and they enforce them. They take appropriate and fair corrective action when they see behaviour that is not appropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

The maintainers can remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that do not obey this code of conduct. They give the reasons for a moderation decision when it is appropriate.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all community spaces. It also applies when a person officially represents the community in public spaces. Examples are an official email address, an official social media account, or an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or other unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers. Until this file gives a private contact address, use the **Report content** menu item of GitHub on the comment, the issue or the discussion. The maintainers review and investigate all complaints quickly and fairly.

All maintainers must respect the privacy and the security of the person who reports an incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainers use these guidelines to decide the result of an action that breaks this code of conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** The use of language that is not appropriate, or other behaviour that is not professional or not welcome in the community.

**Result:** A private, written warning from the maintainers, with a clear statement of the violation and why the behaviour was not appropriate. A public apology can be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** One violation, or a series of actions.

**Result:** A warning with results if the behaviour continues. For a specified period, the person must not interact with the persons involved, and this includes the maintainers. This includes community spaces and external channels, such as social media. If the person does not obey these terms, the result can be a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation of community standards, and this includes sustained behaviour that is not appropriate.

**Result:** A temporary ban from all interaction and public communication with the community, for a specified period. During this period, the person must not interact in public or in private with the persons involved, and this includes the maintainers. If the person does not obey these terms, the result can be a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community impact:** A pattern of violations of community standards. This includes sustained behaviour that is not appropriate, harassment of a person, and aggression toward or disparagement of groups of persons.

**Result:** A permanent ban from all public interaction in the community.
